2. Information we collect

The current intended version of Dino Dash is designed to operate without collecting or transmitting personal information to the developer. The App does not require an account or ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or location.

3. Information stored on your device

Dino Dash stores your best score locally on your device so you can track your progress between sessions. The score is not intentionally transmitted to the developer or a third party by the game. Uninstalling the App or clearing its application data may remove the locally stored score.

4. Advertising and analytics

Dino Dash is intended to be free to play without advertisements. The intended version does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. We do not use the game to build advertising profiles or track your activity across other apps.

5. Sharing of information

The current intended version does not send gameplay or personal information to the developer or third parties. We do not sell or rent personal information. If future versions introduce services that collect or share data, this policy will be updated to explain those practices, as applicable.

6. Internet and device permissions

The game is designed to work offline and does not need access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photos for core gameplay. The operating system may process technical information as part of normal device operation; this policy does not claim the developer receives such information.

7. Children’s privacy

Dino Dash is a casual game that may appeal to a broad audience. The App is not intended to collect personal information from children. If the App is distributed to children or is designed to target children, additional applicable laws and Google Play Families requirements must be followed.

8. Data security and retention

The best score is stored locally within the App’s application data. The developer does not maintain a developer-operated database of player scores in the current intended version. You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.

9. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. The current version will be published at this page.
